# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** PixelForge – Vinayak’s Pixel Art Portfolio
**Owner:** Vinayak Tiwari
**Version:** 1.0
**Platform:** Web Application
**Primary Device:** Desktop-first, fully responsive

---

# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

PixelForge is a modern, creative, and interactive personal portfolio website built with a **pixel art aesthetic**, inspired by retro games. It serves as a professional showcase of Vinayak Tiwari’s skills, projects, and experience in software development and data science.

The website will combine:

* Professional portfolio functionality
* Interactive game-like experience
* Modern frontend technologies
* Pixel art visual style

---

## 1.2 Goals

### Primary Goals

* Showcase projects professionally
* Impress recruiters immediately (within 5–10 seconds)
* Demonstrate strong frontend and full-stack skills
* Differentiate from traditional portfolios
* Increase internship and job opportunities

### Secondary Goals

* Build strong personal brand identity
* Demonstrate creativity and technical depth
* Provide memorable and interactive experience
* Serve as central hub for professional links

---

## 1.3 Success Metrics

* Recruiter engagement time > 30 seconds
* Portfolio load time < 2 seconds
* Lighthouse score > 90
* Increase in internship responses
* Positive feedback from recruiters/developers
